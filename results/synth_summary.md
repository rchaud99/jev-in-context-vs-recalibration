Reading (numbers from the tables below):
- Opus is the best of the three LLMs: lower MSE than Gemini and Jev at k=100 on every task (CI excludes 0 everywhere except Opus − Gemini on task 5), and it roughly ties logistic regression on the additive tasks 1 and 4a/4b.
- Most of that edge is its prior, not learning: on task 1 its zero-shot MSE (0.0232) already equals its k=100 MSE (0.0224), and k=5-20 are worse than zero-shot. Logistic regression goes on to 0.0054 at k=400.
- It does learn the base rate from examples (task 4a: 0.32 → 0.09, task 4b: 0.32 → 0.87 at k=100, like Gemini), but more slowly than simply averaging the labels (at k=5 it is still at 0.29 / 0.60).
- Pure noise (task 3): it spreads its predictions less than Gemini or Jev (SD 0.09 vs 0.19-0.21 at k=100), but still invents case-level signal, so it is significantly worse than base-rate-only (MSE 0.0114 vs 0.0004).
- Interaction (task 2): AUROC 0.56 at k=100 (Bayes 0.73), the same as Gemini and Jev, and worse than base-rate-only on MSE. It does not pick up the rash × treatment interaction from 100 examples.
- Task 1 tellin (which truly raises risk): at k=100 Opus predicts 0.34 for tellin cases vs a true 0.44 (Jev 0.43, Gemini 0.40), so its 'treatment helps' prior resists the examples more than the others' does.
