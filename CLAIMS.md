# Claims ledger: what survived, what was killed, and why

Last updated 2026-09-28. "v1" = the 2026-09-27 study (README at commit 291966f). "v2" = the preregistered re-test on
leak-repaired data (RESEARCH_AGENDA.md).

## Killed or withdrawn claims
| # | Claim (v1 wording, paraphrased) | Why it is killed | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | "The decision model is better" (than general LLMs, or than simple ML), as a general claim | On structured data, in-context examples ≈ logistic regression on the same labels (significantly better on 0/8 at k=50–200). Frontier LLMs had lower Brier on most tasks. | REVIEW_2026-09-27 |
| K2 | "One sentence stating the base rate fixes most of the miscalibration" | Told beat simply predicting the stated rate (a constant) on only 2/8 tasks. The claim was measured against zero-shot, not against the trivial baseline. | results/baseline_audit.md (A) |
| K3 | All Opus 5.5 results (zero-shot AUROC 0.87 on GitHub; "Opus beats Jev on 7/8") | The provider route varied by task (Anthropic vs OpenRouter), with cache keys ignoring the route. Contamination was unchecked (Opus cutoff June 2026; data Jan–Aug 2026). Opus's GitHub AUROC beat a per-repo-rate oracle. Not re-run in v2; withdrawn. | REVIEW_2026-09-27 |
| K4 | Shelter results (e.g. in-context AUROC 0.64 → 0.84) | Label leak: `sex` (spay/neuter status) is recorded after the outcome, and alone gives CV AUROC 0.78. The v2 repair drops it, and the zero-shot AUROC of the decision model falls to 0.53. | v2/leak_audit.py, results/v2/e1_tests.csv |
| K5 | GitHub PR results that rely on the "draft" marker | `draft` is the state at closing (0/48 drafts merged). Repaired in v2. Titles may also have been renamed at merge (not repairable); the sample is the top 200 by relevance, not random. | REVIEW_2026-09-27 |
| K6 | Crimes AUROC 0.78 zero-shot | Inflated by police-initiated offence types that exist because of an arrest (narcotics 98%). On repaired v2 data it is 0.68 for the same model. | results/v2/e1_tests.csv |
| K7 | "Gemini beats Jev zero-shot on 6/8" as evidence of model quality | These are the same 6 tasks where a constant beats Jev. It is a base-rate effect, not better ranking. | REVIEW_2026-09-27 |
| K8 | Synthetic tasks: "LR gets 7–8× closer to the truth" | That is an MSE ratio (≈2.8× in RMSE), and LR is the correctly specified model for data generated by a logistic model. | REVIEW_2026-09-27 |
| K9 | "Stack, don't stuff": the LLM score helps TabPFN | The LLM score doesn't significantly help TabPFN on 7/8; the wins were TabPFN's own. | REVIEW_2026-09-27 |
| K10 | "Platt matters most with noisy labels"; "split the labels when over the context limit" | Never tested (noisy + Platt not run; the context limit was never exceeded). | REVIEW_2026-09-27 |
| K11 | "Shuffled-label control shows learning on 8/8" | In-context minus zero-shot AUROC is significant on only 5/8 (not crimes, flights or HN). | REVIEW_2026-09-27 |
| K12 | Criticism of the decision model's vendor ("publishes no ECE…") | Out of scope for a neutral guide and not a finding. Removed by user decision. | — |

## v2 verdicts (preregistered; RESEARCH_AGENDA.md)
| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | **Telling an LLM the base rate in its prompt gives worse Brier than predicting that rate as a constant** (E1-R R1) | **Confirmed**: 8/9 configurations in the predicted (+) direction, and Gemini in its predicted (−) direction. All are Holm-significant and all 3 seeds agree. It also held post hoc in E1 (8 tasks). | results/v2/replication_table.md |
| V2 | **Applying the same number in code (a label-free logit shift) beats telling the model** (E1-R R2) | **Confirmed**: 9/9 > 0 after Holm (Gemini was predicted null; +0.004). Across 117 cells, told is never better after Holm; shift is better in 47. | same |
| V3 | E1 primary: shift beats told on ≥ 5/8 tasks per model | **Failed** for 7/9 configurations (only GPT-4o-mini and Llama pass) | results/v2/e1_summary.md |
| V4 | v1 S5 at its stated strength ("5/8 tasks for Jev") | **Refuted**: 3/8 on the repaired data. The direction holds when pooled. | same |
| V5 | P2: Gemini null on told vs shift | Confirmed in E1; in E1-R a tiny positive (+0.004 [+0.000, +0.008]) | same |
| V6 | P5: among cells with zero-shot AUROC ≥ 0.65, shift < constant in ≥ 70%, and shift is significantly worse than the constant only where AUROC < 0.6 | **Refuted**: 64% (E1) / 73% (E1-R). Shift is significantly worse than the constant even at AUROC 0.64–0.70 (Llama, Qwen and DeepSeek-verbal on crimes; Llama on NYC 311), because logprob scores are too extreme for a 1-parameter fix. | e1_summary.md / e1r_summary.md, P5 |
| V7 | P6: Platt(100) beats shift for logprob models | Confirmed (38/117 cells better, 2 worse) | replication_table.md |
| V8 | "Told < constant" holds regardless of the model's ranking skill | **Killed**: at zero-shot AUROC > 0.75, told beats or ties the constant (5 cells). The claim is scoped to low-signal tasks. | replication_table.md (AUROC bands) E2 adds a counterexample: DeepSeek-V4-Pro on NYC 311 (AUROC 0.80) is told-worse than the constant by +0.13. |
| V9 | P4: with wordings w1 and w2, significant-task counts stay within ±2 of w0 | Holds for 8/9. Jev fails in the direction of the claim (w0 2, w1 4, w2 5 tasks where shift is better): the "Important calibration information…" wording makes telling worse. The sign never flips. | e1_tests.csv, P4 |
| V10 | E2 (DeepSeek-V4-Pro, logprob): E2a told − shift > 0 on both datasets; E2b told − const > 0 because its mean AUROC is < 0.70 (0.59 / 0.61) | **Confirmed** both: E1 +0.030 / +0.051; E1-R +0.029 [+0.019, +0.038] / +0.042 [+0.033, +0.051]; all seeds agree | results/v2/e1*_e2_posthoc_pooled.csv |
| V11 | E3: reasoning models (GPT-5-mini at medium effort; DeepSeek-V4-Flash with thinking) have told − shift > 0 and told − const > 0 on both datasets | **Confirmed**: all 8 pooled differences > 0 (p ≤ 0.008), and all seeds agree | results/v2/extensions_e2_e3.csv |
